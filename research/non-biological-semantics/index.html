Research Programme

Non-Biological Semantics (NBS)

A proposed field-level research programme for the study of semantic organisation in artificial systems.

2 public papersLatest publication · 26 September 2026

Non-Biological Semantics separates claims about artificial representations into distinct evidential levels rather than treating measurable structure, causal relevance and human interpretation as interchangeable. The programme begins with a foundational methodological paper and now includes its first controlled empirical sequence.

The publications below are presented as separate, permanent papers within the same research programme. Later papers may extend, test or revise the programme without altering the published record of earlier work.
